🔎 Research Guide

A simple guide for law students to conduct effective legal research.

1. Identify the Legal Issue

Clearly understand the legal question before starting your research.

2. Find Relevant Laws

Check the Constitution, Acts, Rules and other applicable legal provisions.

3. Search Case Laws

Find important Supreme Court and High Court judgments related to your issue.

4. Check Legal Sources

  • Constitution of India
  • Central and State Acts
  • Supreme Court Judgments
  • High Court Judgments
  • Legal Journals and Articles

5. Prepare Your Notes

Write down the important provisions, facts, issues, judgments and legal principles.
