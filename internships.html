Internships

Explore internship opportunities and guidance for law students.

Where to Find Internships

  • Law firms and legal chambers
  • Corporate legal departments
  • NGOs and legal aid organisations
  • Courts and tribunals
  • Government legal departments

How to Apply

  • Prepare a professional CV.
  • Write a short internship application.
  • Attach relevant academic documents.
  • Apply through official websites or email.
  • Follow up professionally after applying.

Important Tips

Start applying early, keep your CV updated, and maintain a record of applications and responses.
