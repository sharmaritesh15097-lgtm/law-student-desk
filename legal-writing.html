✍️ Legal Writing

A simple guide to help law students improve their legal writing skills.

1. Understand the Question

Read the legal problem carefully and identify the main issues involved.

2. Use Legal Provisions

Support your answer with relevant sections, Articles, Rules and legal principles.

3. Use Case Laws

Include relevant judgments to support your legal arguments.

4. Structure Your Answer

  • Introduction
  • Issues
  • Applicable Law
  • Case Laws
  • Analysis
  • Conclusion

5. Write Clearly

Use simple, precise and professional legal language. Avoid unnecessary repetition.
