Important Case Notes

Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala

Facts: The case concerned the extent of Parliament's power to amend the Constitution.

Issue: Whether Parliament can change the basic structure of the Constitution.

Judgment: The Supreme Court established the Basic Structure Doctrine.

Legal Principle: Parliament cannot destroy the basic structure of the Constitution.

Maneka Gandhi v. Union of India

Facts: The case concerned the right to personal liberty under Article 21.

Issue: Whether the procedure affecting personal liberty must be fair and reasonable.

Judgment: The Supreme Court expanded the protection of Article 21.

Legal Principle: Personal liberty must be protected through a fair and reasonable procedure.

Minerva Mills v. Union of India

Facts: The case challenged constitutional amendments affecting the amending power.

Issue: Whether Parliament has unlimited power to amend the Constitution.

Judgment: The Supreme Court reaffirmed the Basic Structure Doctrine.

Legal Principle: Parliament's amending power is limited.
