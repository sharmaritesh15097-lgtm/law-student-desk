CV & LinkedIn

Build a professional CV and improve your LinkedIn profile for legal internships, opportunities and career growth.

CV Essentials

  • Keep your CV clear and professional.
  • Add your education and law degree details.
  • Mention internships and relevant experience.
  • Include legal skills, certifications and achievements.
  • Keep the CV concise and easy to read.

LinkedIn Profile

  • Use a professional profile photo.
  • Add a clear headline related to your legal career.
  • Write a short and professional About section.
  • Add education, internships, skills and certifications.
  • Connect with lawyers, law firms and legal professionals.

Important Tip

Keep your CV and LinkedIn profile updated whenever you gain a new skill, internship, certificate or achievement.
