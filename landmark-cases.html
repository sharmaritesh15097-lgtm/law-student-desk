Landmark Cases

Important Landmark Judgments

Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala

Principle: Basic Structure Doctrine.

The Supreme Court held that Parliament can amend the Constitution but cannot destroy its basic structure.

Maneka Gandhi v. Union of India

Principle: Protection of personal liberty.

The case expanded the interpretation of Article 21 and strengthened the protection of individual liberty.

Minerva Mills v. Union of India

Principle: Limited amending power.

The Supreme Court reaffirmed that the basic structure of the Constitution cannot be destroyed.
